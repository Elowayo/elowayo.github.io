# Elowa – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Elowa ("we," "us," "our") handles information when you use the Elowa mobile application (the "App"). We built Elowa around a simple principle: your face is yours, so your check-in photos stay on your device by default and are never uploaded to us.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account information (collected by us)
When you sign up using single sign-on (SSO) through Supabase, our authentication and backend infrastructure provider, we collect your email address and full name. This information is used to create and secure your account, authenticate you across sessions, and communicate with you about the App (such as service updates or support responses). We also store, against your account, the record of your agreement to the in-app notice described in Section 1.4 — which version you agreed to, and when.

**The skin concerns you select.** When you choose which skin concerns to track (for example acne, redness, pigmentation), that selection is stored against your account as well as on your device, so that it is restored if you reinstall the App or move to a new phone. We treat this as health-related information about you, and it is held for that single purpose: no other feature reads it, it is never used for advertising or profiling, it is never shared with anyone outside Supabase, and — as Section 1.4 sets out — it is specifically excluded from the usage and diagnostic data we collect. Your device's copy is the one the App works from; the copy held against your account exists only to restore it. Erasing your data or closing your account removes both copies (see Section 5).

We rely on your consent for storing your concern selections, given through the in-app notice shown when you set up your account. That consent covers your concern selections and nothing else: the usage and diagnostic data in Section 1.4 is asked for as a separate choice, and agreeing to one does not agree you to the other. You can withdraw this consent by removing your concerns in Settings, by erasing your data, or by closing your account.

### 1.2 Check-in photos and notes (not collected by us)
Your daily check-in photos — the three guided facial photos — and any notes you attach to a check-in are stored locally on your device only. We do not receive, transmit, view, or store these photos or notes on our servers as part of the App's normal operation. We have no server-side copy of your photos and no ability to view them.

### 1.3 Device backups
Elowa stores your check-in photos and notes in the App's own storage area on your device. It does not add them to your phone's photo library, so they do not appear in, and are not uploaded by, iCloud Photos or Google Photos.

Your phone's own backup system is a separate matter, and it works differently on each platform.

**On iOS**, the App's storage area is included in iCloud Backup, which is switched on by default on most iPhones. Where it is active, your check-in photos and notes are copied to Apple's servers as part of that backup. This is performed by iOS, not by Elowa: we do not initiate it, cannot see whether it has run, and have no access to what it holds. It is governed by your agreement with Apple, and you can change what iCloud Backup includes in your device's settings.

**On Android**, Elowa is configured so that its data is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup. Your check-in photos and notes are not copied to Google's servers. They are included when you transfer directly from one phone to another during device setup, which does not involve storing them in the cloud.

In both cases the mechanism belongs to the operating system rather than to us. We do not receive a copy of your check-in photos or notes through any of it, and we cannot restore them for you.

### 1.4 Usage and diagnostic data
We collect limited technical and usage data to keep the App working, secure it, and improve it. This is what we collect, specifically:

- **Technical data:** app version, device type, operating system version, and crash reports.
- **Performance data:** how long a check-in takes to save, and when saving one fails.
- **Your check-ins:** that you completed one and on what calendar date, how long it took, how many photos it held, whether it was your first, and — if you left partway through — how far you had got.
- **Features and settings you use:** that you opened the comparison view and how you reached it, that you turned the reframing assist on or off, that you reset the alignment reference, that you finished onboarding, that you erased your data, and the overlay level your check-in used.

We ask for your agreement to this collection as its own separate choice when you first set up your account — not as part of the health-data notice described in Section 1.1, and not bundled with anything else. It is off unless you turn it on, and declining it has no effect on your access to the App or on any other part of your account. You can turn it on or off at any time in Settings, and the App works exactly the same either way. Turning it off stops new records being created; it does not delete records already collected — closing your account does that (see Section 5).

**Crash reports are the exception.** If the App crashes, a crash report is sent whether or not you have usage measurement turned on. It describes the failure — where in the code it happened, and on what device and App version — and never contains any of the content listed below. We keep it outside the choice because an app that crashes on your phone cannot be fixed if we never learn that it did.

**What this data never includes:** your check-in photos, or anything derived from them; your notes — not what they say, not how long they are, not even whether you wrote one; the skin concerns you selected, or how many you picked; your photo quality scores; the time of day you use the App; or your location. Nothing we collect here describes what your skin looks like or what you are concerned about. Your concern selections are stored separately, under Section 1.1, and are never joined to this data.

**How long we keep it.** These records are kept while your account is open, and are deleted when you close it. We are not able to promise a fixed deletion date for records held while your account is open: our analytics provider guarantees a minimum storage period rather than deleting on a schedule, so we would rather tell you the mechanism that actually applies — deletion on account closure — than quote a timetable we do not control.

### 1.5 Processing that happens only on your device (not collected by us)
To help you take comparable photos, the App performs limited processing on your device while you are using it. We do not receive the results of it.

- **While the camera is open**, the App reads the live camera preview to estimate overall brightness and colour temperature, and reads your phone's motion sensors to estimate the angle you are holding the phone at. This is how the App tells you that the light or the angle has shifted since your last check-in. It measures the scene, not your skin. The preview frames it reads are not saved, and the estimates are not retained after the check-in.
- **After a photo is taken**, if you have turned on the reframing assist ("Line your photos up" in Settings), the App locates the face in the photo and adjusts the crop so that your face appears at a consistent size and position. Only the resulting photo is saved, and it is saved on your device.

**This processing does not identify you.** Locating a face in a frame is not the same as recognising whose face it is. The App does not create, collect, or store a faceprint, facial template, or any other biometric identifier, and it does not attempt to match you against any photo, database, or previous check-in.

None of this processing transmits anything to us or to any third party. Because it happens entirely on your device and produces nothing we receive, there is nothing here for us to store, share, or delete on your behalf.

### 1.6 Information we do not collect
We do not collect your precise or approximate location, payment card details (if applicable, these are handled by the app store or a payment processor, not stored by us), or any health records from a healthcare provider. As described in Section 1.2, we do not collect or store check-in photos at all — not the account holder's, and not anyone else's. Elowa is designed for a single account holder to photograph themselves; our Terms of Service require that you not use the App to photograph anyone else without their knowledge and consent, but because we never receive any check-in photos, we have no visibility into or ability to monitor how the App's camera is used.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to create and maintain your account, authenticate your sign-in, provide, operate, and improve the App's features, respond to support requests and communicate service-related updates, and detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or misuse. We do not use your check-in photos for any purpose, because we do not have them.

**Our legal bases (EU/UK users).** Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following:

- **Your account details (email, name):** performance of our contract with you — we cannot give you an account without them.
- **Your skin concern selections:** your explicit consent, which you give through the in-app notice and can withdraw at any time.
- **The record of your agreement to that notice:** our legitimate interest in being able to show what you agreed to and when, and our legal obligation to keep that record.
- **Usage and diagnostic data:** your consent, given as a separate choice and withdrawable at any time in Settings.
- **Security, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting:** our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and secure, balanced against your rights.

## 3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances: with Supabase, our authentication and backend infrastructure provider, which stores and processes your email, name, consent record, and concern selections so that your account works across devices, and is bound by its own data protection obligations; with our product analytics and diagnostics provider, PostHog, which hosts this data in the European Union and processes it only on our instructions, limited to the technical and usage data described in Section 1.4; if required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Elowa, our users, or the public; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you before your account information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We never have access to your check-in photos, so there is nothing of that kind for us to share, sell, or lose in a breach of our systems.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Account data — email, name, consent record, and concern selections — is stored and processed by Supabase, which employs industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit. Access to your account data is restricted at the database level so that only your own signed-in account can read or change it. Check-in photos and notes remain on your device, protected by your device's own security (passcode, biometric lock, encryption), which we recommend you keep enabled. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of the account data we do hold.

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

The App offers two separate actions, and the difference between them matters:

- **Erase your data** removes your check-in photos, notes, concerns, scores, and every setting you have changed, from your device — and also removes the copy of your concern selections held against your account (Section 1.1). Because everything else in that list lives only on your device, there is no other copy for us to delete. Your account stays open. Two consequences are worth stating plainly: **the usage and diagnostic records described in Section 1.4 are not affected** — they are not on your device, and erasing does not reach them — and because your settings are part of what is erased, any preference you had changed, including the usage-measurement setting in Section 1.4, returns to its default. Usage measurement is off unless you turn it on, so erasing your data stops it; the App asks you about it again when you set your check-ins up afresh. The record of your agreement to the in-app notice is not part of what erasing removes — it is the receipt for what you agreed to and when, and it stays until you close your account.
- **Close your account** removes everything, in both places: your on-device data, your account information (email, name, your concern selections, and the dated record of your agreement to the in-app notice), and the usage and diagnostic records held by us and our analytics provider. If a closure does not complete, we record that one failed — the reason only, with no reference to you or to any account, so that people cannot end up trapped in an account they are trying to leave without us knowing. This is available inside the App, cannot be undone, and we hold no copy to restore from.

While a closure is being completed we briefly hold your account identifier on its own, and nothing else, so that the deletion of your usage data can be retried if our analytics provider cannot be reached at that moment. It is deleted as soon as that succeeds, and it exists only to finish the erasure you asked for.

You can control whether your device backs up your photos to a third-party cloud service through that service's own settings (e.g., your phone's backup settings) — this is outside the App and outside our control.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing, and under the California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA and similar U.S. state laws the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because we do not sell personal information, there is no "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising to opt out of. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello.elowa@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

**Withdrawing consent.** Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time — by removing your concerns in Settings, by turning off usage measurement, by erasing your data, or by closing your account. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of what we did before you withdrew it.

**No automated decision-making.** We do not make decisions about you by automated means that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects, and we do not profile you. The App's comparisons are shown to you on your own device; they are not decisions we make about you.

**Complaints.** If you are in the EU, the UK, or another region with a data protection authority, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office — in addition to contacting us. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first, but you are not required to come to us before going to them.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Elowa is not directed at minors. You must be 18, or the age of legal majority where you live if that is higher, to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect account information from anyone below that age. We do not currently use any age-verification system, and rely on the representation you make when creating an account. If we become aware — through a report, complaint, or other means — that we've collected account data from someone under the applicable minimum age, we will take reasonable steps to delete that data within a reasonable time.

## 7. International Users

Elowa may be used by people in different countries. Account data — your email, name, consent record, and concern selections — is stored and processed by Supabase in the United States, in Amazon Web Services' US East region (Northern Virginia). If you are located outside the United States, using Elowa means your account data is transferred to and held there.

Because your concern selections are health-related, we treat that transfer as significant. Supabase is bound by a data processing agreement with us and implements safeguards for international transfers as required by applicable law, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where they apply. Your usage and diagnostic data is separate and is held in the European Union by PostHog, as described in Section 3.

Because check-in photos never leave your device, they are not subject to cross-border transfer through our systems.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your account information, including your concern selections, for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Your concern selections are removed as soon as you erase your data or close your account, whichever comes first. Check-in photos and notes are retained on your device for as long as you choose to keep them there; we have no visibility into or control over this.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, our practices, or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes through the App or by email, and will update the "Last updated" date above. Where a change affects what we collect or how we handle your information, the App shows you the revised notice and asks you to agree to it again; collection under the new version does not begin until you do, and your agreement is recorded against your account with its date and version.
