Is my skin actually improving?

Elowa answers that with a two-minute daily photo check-in — three guided poses, an honest before-and-after comparison, and photos that stay on your phone.

Get in touch How your data is handled

How a check-in works

The same three photos, taken the same way, every day. Consistency is what makes a comparison worth looking at.

1

Line up

On-screen guides hold your angle, distance, and framing steady. Elowa reads the live preview for brightness and colour temperature, and your phone's motion sensors for tilt, so it can tell you when the light or the angle has drifted since last time.

2

Three poses

Front, left, right. It takes about two minutes. Add a note if the day is worth remembering — a new product, a flare-up, a bad night's sleep.

3

Compare honestly

Wipe between any two days, or view them side by side. When a comparison may be unreliable, Elowa says so instead of quietly showing you a flattering one.

Your face is yours

Privacy isn't a setting in Elowa — it's the architecture. There is no server-side copy of your photos, because they are never sent anywhere.

Photos stay on your device

Check-in photos and notes are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. We never receive, view, or store them.

Processing happens on your phone

Lighting and angle checks, and the optional reframing assist, all run on-device. Nothing from them is transmitted.

We hold only your account

Your name, email, your consent record, and the skin concerns you chose — so they come back when you change phones. Nothing else.

Measurement you opt into

Off unless you turn it on. Limited technical and usage data, never touching your photos, notes, or concerns — and the app works identically either way.

Elowa is a tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or skin condition, and nothing in the app is medical or dermatological advice. If you have a concern about your skin, a mole, or a lesion, see a licensed dermatologist or physician.
